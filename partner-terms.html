Star69AI Partner Program Terms

These terms govern participation in the Star69AI referral partner program operated by Solomon Jarman, an individual sole proprietor using the Star69AI service brand (“Star69AI,” “we,” or “us”). By submitting an application and checking the partner-program consent box, or by continuing to participate after accepting these terms, you agree to this agreement. Applications are subject to review and approval; applying does not guarantee acceptance.

Commission summary: 10% for 1–4 active paying referred customers, 15% for 5–14, and 20% for 15 or more. The maximum commission rate is 20%. Commissions apply only to qualifying subscription-plan payments actually collected after discounts—not to every amount a referred customer spends.

1. Commission tiers

Your commission tier is determined automatically by the number of your attributed customers who are converted, active and paying at the time a qualifying subscription payment is processed:

  • 1–4 active paying referred customers: 10%.
  • 5–14 active paying referred customers: 15%.
  • 15 or more active paying referred customers: 20%.

The 20% rate is an absolute cap. Trialing, past-due, suspended, canceled, refunded or otherwise non-paying accounts do not count as active paying referrals. The applicable rate is determined for each qualifying payment using the active paying referral count at that time. A later tier change does not retroactively recalculate previously credited commissions.

2. Eligible subscription revenue

Commission is earned only on the actual amount Star69AI successfully collects for an eligible subscription plan, after customer discounts and before no other excluded amounts are included. Eligible billing schedules include monthly subscriptions and prepaid six-month or twelve-month subscription plans. For prepaid plans, commission is calculated on the eligible subscription amount actually collected in the prepaid transaction.

No commission is earned on list prices, unpaid invoices, failed transactions, amounts not received by Star69AI, or any transaction that is voided, refunded, reversed or charged back.

3. Excluded revenue

The following are never commissionable, whether purchased separately or alongside a subscription:

  • One-time setup, onboarding or activation fees.
  • Add-ons, optional upgrades or feature purchases.
  • Custom development, custom AI workflows, integrations, consulting, configuration or other custom work.
  • Intelligent Directory Operator projects or directory synchronization and maintenance work.
  • SMS, messaging, telephony, usage overages, pay-as-you-go usage or other usage-based charges.
  • Third-party provider charges, carrier charges, pass-through expenses, reimbursable costs or dedicated resource costs.
  • Taxes, government fees, refunds, credits, chargebacks, reversals or uncollected amounts.

4. Customer discounts and offers

A standard partner referral link tracks and credits an eligible purchase to the referring partner. The customer pays the regular advertised subscription-plan price, with no customer discount, and the partner's applicable commission is calculated on the full subscription amount Star69AI actually collects. A discounted partner referral link may offer 15% off eligible monthly subscription payments for the first 12 months only; for those payments, the partner's applicable commission is calculated on the discounted subscription amount actually collected, not the original list price. Example: if a $249 monthly plan is purchased through a standard link, the customer pays $249 and commission is calculated on $249. If the same plan is purchased through an eligible discounted link, the customer pays $211.65 and commission is calculated on $211.65. The one-time setup fee is not discounted and is never commissionable.

Partner customer discounts cannot be combined with AI Challenge rewards or six-month/twelve-month prepaid offers. The customer must qualify for and select one available offer. Partner commission is calculated on the subscription amount actually collected after any applicable discount.

5. Referral attribution

Referral attribution is intended to remain valid for 90 days after a tracked referral interaction when tracking is operating and the referral can be verified. Star69AI may review attribution records and reject self-referrals, duplicate referrals, existing customers, fraudulent activity, manipulated tracking or referrals that cannot reasonably be verified. A referral link or click does not itself create a commission; a qualifying subscription payment must be successfully collected.

6. Validation and payout

  • Each eligible commission is subject to a 30-day validation hold beginning from the qualifying payment date.
  • Commissions become payable only after the hold expires and the amount remains eligible after review.
  • The minimum payout balance is $50 in cleared, payable commissions.
  • Star69AI currently reviews and issues partner payouts manually through PayPal, generally on a monthly processing cycle. Partners must maintain a valid PayPal email address.
  • Refunds, chargebacks, reversals, duplicate credits or accounting corrections may be deducted from unpaid balances or recovered from future commissions where permitted by law.

A commission shown as pending is not yet payable. A dashboard balance is an accounting display and is not a guarantee of immediate payment.

7. Partner responsibilities and conduct

  • Clearly disclose your material connection to Star69AI when promoting the service.
  • Use accurate, current descriptions and only approved claims. Do not guarantee revenue, leads, call outcomes, savings or business results.
  • Comply with applicable advertising, privacy, consumer-protection, email, text-message and anti-spam laws.
  • Do not use spam, deceptive advertising, impersonation, misleading earnings claims, unauthorized brand use or unlawful solicitation.
  • Do not make commitments, change pricing, promise features or represent yourself as an employee or authorized agent of Star69AI.
  • Do not submit self-referrals, fake leads, duplicate accounts or referrals created through fraud or policy evasion.

8. Independent relationship

You participate as an independent referral partner. This agreement does not create employment, a legal partnership, joint venture, franchise, fiduciary relationship or authority to bind Star69AI. You are responsible for your own business expenses, records and applicable tax obligations. Star69AI does not provide tax advice.

9. Program changes and termination

Star69AI may modify, suspend or terminate the partner program or a partner’s participation prospectively, including for inactivity, misconduct, fraud, legal or operational requirements. We will communicate material changes where reasonably practicable. Changes do not authorize retroactive reductions to commissions already earned under the terms then in effect, except for corrections, refunds, chargebacks, fraud or other reversals described above and as permitted by law. Upon termination, no new commissions accrue after the effective termination date; properly earned and validated commissions remain subject to the payout rules in this agreement.

10. Records and questions

Star69AI maintains referral and commission records to support attribution, payment reconciliation and program administration. Partners should review their Partner Center and promptly report suspected discrepancies to support@star69ai.com.

11. Agreement and legal review

This agreement is intended to describe the current Star69AI Partner Program and does not guarantee that any particular marketing method, tax treatment or legal result applies to every partner. Applicable law may impose additional requirements. Partners should obtain independent legal and tax advice where appropriate.
