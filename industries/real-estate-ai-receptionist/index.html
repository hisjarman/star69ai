REAL ESTATE CALL WORKFLOWS

Capture the context. Route the opportunity.

Keep listing facts current, avoid steering callers and use clear human handoffs for decisions that need an agent or property manager.

Buyer and seller leads

Capture area, property type, budget range if volunteered, timeline and preferred contact method.

Listing questions

Collect the listing reference and question, then route to the assigned agent. Only share listing facts approved and kept current by the brokerage.

Rental and property management

Separate tenant, prospective renter, owner and vendor calls, with clear routing for maintenance and leasing requests.

Viewing requests

Capture preferred windows and pass them to the agent or connected calendar; do not promise availability until confirmed.
