YOUR MINUTES · YOUR VAULT

Unused minutes keep working for you.

50% of unused included voice minutes carry into your private Minute Vault while your subscription remains active and paid. Each month’s included minutes are used first; once those are used, the oldest vaulted minutes are applied before billable overage begins. New calls stop at a hard ceiling of 75% overage of the plan’s included minutes: 225 additional minutes on Starter, 450 on Growth and 900 on Pro.

MINUTE VAULTMinutes, not money
  • 50% of unused included minutes carry forward without monthly expiry while subscribed.
  • Vault balance is preserved when you upgrade or downgrade; future monthly grants follow the new plan.
  • Minutes cannot be sold, transferred, cashed out or converted into account credit.
IMPORTANT ACCOUNT TERMSSubscription-linked benefit
  • Vaulted minutes have no monetary or refund value.
  • All remaining vaulted minutes are forfeited when service is cancelled.
  • After included and vaulted minutes are exhausted, usage may be billed at the plan’s published overage rate up to the hard 75% plan-minute ceiling; new calls stop at that ceiling.

Minute Vault balances are tracked by Star69AI in the client portal. The balance is a usage benefit only, not stored value, a payment instrument, or a promise of cash equivalence. Overage estimates remain estimates until metered billing is activated and verified.
