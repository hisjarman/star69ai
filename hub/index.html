What a good industry setup defines

  • What the receptionist may answer—and what it must not guess.
  • Which details to collect before a callback or appointment request.
  • When to transfer, escalate or create a follow-up task.
  • How urgent, sensitive or out-of-scope calls reach a person.
  • How the team reviews call outcomes and improves the flow.
