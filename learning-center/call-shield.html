How it works

Call Shield checks repeated attempts from the same caller to the same business number within a configurable time window. When the attempt threshold is exceeded, later attempts can be rejected during a cooldown before they connect to the AI agent.

Before the AI answers

Screening runs before the inbound call is connected to the configured AI receptionist.

Business-level controls

Authorized clients can choose bounded repeat-attempt, time-window, cooldown and maximum AI call-duration settings.

Managed safeguards

Star69AI retains server-side limits, usage protections and review of permanent caller blocks.

Clients can adjust the available numeric limits in Mission Control. Current source-level ranges are 3–20 attempts, a 1–60 minute detection window, a 1–120 minute cooldown and a 1–30 minute maximum AI call duration. The effective call duration may be shorter when plan-minute or wallet reservations require it.

What Call Shield is — and isn't

  • It is a repeat-attempt screening control designed to reduce unnecessary AI-agent conversations from repeated callers.
  • It is not a guarantee against spam, robocalls, harassment or every unwanted call.
  • It cannot reliably detect caller-ID spoofing.
  • Withheld or invalid caller IDs are not automatically blocked by the repeat-caller rule.
  • A rejected call may still incur carrier or telephony delivery charges.
  • AI caller-risk labels are advisory; they do not automatically create permanent blocks.

Privacy by design: Call Shield uses a privacy-preserving caller fingerprint scoped to the customer and dialed business number. Raw caller phone numbers are not written to the Call Shield screening tables. Operational screening state and events are retained for up to 30 days.

Your controls. Our safeguards.

Clients can adjust their permitted repeat-attempt threshold, detection window, cooldown and maximum AI call duration. The Star69AI Crew can review and override account settings when support or safety requires it.

Client settings cannot disable screening, bypass account authorization, raise the 75% plan overage ceiling or automatically create a permanent denylist. Permanent caller blocking requires Crew review. This balances flexibility with predictable service and usage protections.

Frequently asked questions

Will it block every spam call?

No. It focuses on repeated attempts from the same caller identity as presented to the phone system. Different numbers, withheld caller IDs and spoofed caller IDs can limit what it recognizes.

Can I choose how many times someone can call?

Yes, within the bounded range available in Mission Control. Your account displays the currently saved values.

Can I choose the cooldown?

Yes, within the permitted range. After cooldown, a later attempt may be screened again under the current settings.

Can I permanently block a caller?

Permanent blocks require Star69AI Crew review; client settings do not automatically add permanent denylist entries.

Does a rejected call cost nothing?

Not necessarily. Screening prevents connection to the AI-agent conversation, but carrier or telephony delivery charges may still apply.

Does the maximum call duration guarantee that much time?

No. It is an upper limit. Plan minutes, wallet balance and reservations may impose a shorter limit.

Where can existing clients change settings?

Sign in to Mission Control and open Call Shield. If the page or controls are unavailable, contact Crew Support.
