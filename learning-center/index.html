Simplified estimate only. It excludes lead overlap, seasonality, capacity, acquisition costs and callers who would return through another channel. A receptionist cannot guarantee recovery.

Build your own baseline before you buy.

  • Review a representative 30-day call log and count unanswered, abandoned and after-hours calls.
  • Separate sales inquiries from existing-customer support, spam and wrong numbers.
  • Measure how many missed callers later reached the business through another channel.
  • Estimate gross profit—not revenue—for work you could realistically serve.
  • After launch, compare like-for-like periods and track transfers, qualified leads, bookings and complaints.
