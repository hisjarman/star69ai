How to compare AI receptionist pricing

Do not compare a 100-minute plan directly with a 100-call plan. A provider that charges by call may count a 20-second call and a 10-minute call as one unit, while a minute-based plan can run out quickly during long conversations. Also check whether the monthly fee includes a phone number, transfers, SMS, calendar booking, human fallback, integrations, support and ongoing agent maintenance.

For a managed service, ask who builds the call flow, configures business knowledge, tests edge cases, monitors performance and maintains the receptionist after launch. “Setup included” can mean anything from a guided wizard to a provider doing the full implementation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a managed AI receptionist?

A managed provider configures and supports the phone agent for the business. The scope can include call-flow design, business knowledge, integrations, testing, monitoring and ongoing changes. Confirm which tasks are actually included.

How are AI receptionist services billed?

Common models include monthly minute bundles, per-call plans, per-unique-caller plans, flat monthly subscriptions, usage credits and custom quotes. Some combine a monthly fee with overage charges.

Why are some prices marked “Confirm”?

Not every provider publishes a full plan table or overage rate. We do not invent missing prices. Contact the provider and ask for the full recurring cost, setup fee, usage rate and tax treatment.
