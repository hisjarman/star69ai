    01 · GETTING STARTED

    Welcome to your managed receptionist

    What does “managed by Star69AI” mean?

    Star69AI does the technical setup and ongoing configuration for you. We work with you to understand your business, prepare the receptionist’s instructions, configure the call experience, test the agreed workflow and help maintain it. You do not need to build prompts, connect software or operate an AI platform yourself.

    Your Crew remains responsible for real-world business decisions, accurate business information, staffing and responding to callers when a human follow-up is needed.

    What happens before my receptionist goes live?
    1. We confirm your business details, services, hours, service area and call-handling preferences.
    2. We agree on the greeting, questions, lead details to collect and when a call should be transferred or escalated.
    3. Our Crew configures the agent and phone routing, then tests the agreed scenarios.
    4. You review the experience and approve the launch plan.

    A portal account or demo does not by itself mean a phone number is assigned or live call routing is active. Check the Receptionist page or contact support if you are unsure of your launch status.

    What should I prepare for onboarding?

    Have your business name, public phone number, opening hours, service locations, services offered, frequently asked questions, emergency rules, appointment process and human escalation contacts ready. Also identify what information you want collected from callers and who should receive new-lead notifications.

    Only provide information you are authorized to share. Never put passwords, payment-card details or sensitive customer records in ordinary support forms.

    02 · YOUR AI RECEPTIONIST

    What it can do—and how it learns your business

    How does the receptionist handle an incoming call?

    When a call reaches the assigned Star69AI phone setup, the configured voice agent greets the caller and follows the business-specific instructions. Depending on your approved configuration, it can answer common questions, understand why someone is calling, collect contact and job details, qualify a lead, assist with appointment requests and route or escalate calls.

    The exact behavior depends on your setup, available integrations and the instructions we have tested. It should not be assumed to perform every task automatically.

    Can it answer questions about my services and business?

    Yes, for information you have approved and that has been added to its business knowledge and instructions. Examples include service areas, business hours, services offered, basic policies and common questions.

    Tell us whenever information changes. Our Crew reviews and publishes approved updates; the client configuration view is read-only so an untested edit cannot unexpectedly change live calls.

    Can it book appointments or connect to my CRM?

    Appointment requests and supported calendar, CRM or workflow connections may be configured where the required integration is available and approved. Do not assume a calendar is connected or that an appointment is confirmed unless your specific workflow has been configured and tested.

    Use Receptionist → Request a configuration change to describe the system and the outcome you want. We will confirm feasibility, any third-party costs and the test plan before work begins.

    Can it transfer calls to me or my team?

    Call routing and escalation can be designed around your instructions, such as sending urgent calls to an on-call person or directing callers to the right department. The destinations, availability rules and fallback behavior must be configured and tested for your account.

    Keep escalation phone numbers and on-call schedules current. Contact support promptly if a transfer destination changes.

    Can I change the voice, greeting, language or behavior?

    Yes—send a configuration request from the Receptionist page. You can ask about voice style, greeting, language behavior, FAQs, safety rules, privacy handling, call routing and other workflow changes. Our Crew reviews the request and may provide a written quote if additional work or third-party charges apply.

    Star69AI supports configuring multilingual experiences, including a broad range of languages. The languages and quality available for a particular voice, workflow or provider can vary; ask us to confirm the languages needed for your business.

    Why is the configuration page read-only?

    Your portal shows business-facing settings and published instructions for transparency. Star69AI manages the live agent so changes can be reviewed, tested and released safely. Request an edit rather than changing live instructions yourself. Provider credentials, internal security rules and private operating details are never displayed.

    03 · RECEPTIONIST INTELLIGENCE & LEADS

    Turn call patterns into better service

    What does Receptionist Intelligence do?

    It analyzes completed call information to assess caller intent, call outcome, lead-detail completeness, friction points and possible improvements. Findings are displayed as AI assessments to help our Crew review patterns and improve your receptionist.

    Analysis depends on the call event and information supplied by the voice provider. A call can exist in Call activity without a completed Intelligence report.

    Does an AI-identified lead mean I have a customer?

    No. An AI assessment can identify a potential business inquiry, but it does not prove the caller is qualified, will buy, has booked an appointment or generated revenue. Your staff should review the call and confirm the next action.

    What is the difference between an inquiry, lead and booking?
    • Inquiry: someone contacted your business.
    • Potential lead: the conversation appears to concern a service your business may provide and contains useful follow-up context.
    • Qualified lead: your business has reviewed the details and confirmed it meets your own criteria.
    • Appointment request: the caller asked for a time or visit.
    • Confirmed booking: an authoritative connected calendar or authorized person confirms the appointment.
    • Won job/revenue: your business system confirms the sale and amount.
    Will Intelligence automatically change my live receptionist?

    No. Intelligence can prepare recommendations and proposed prompt improvements. Star69AI reviews and tests changes before publishing. Material changes to business policies, pricing, appointments, emergency handling, transfers or privacy require the appropriate human and customer approval.

    What should my staff do with a potential lead?
    1. Open the call details and review the available summary, transcript or recording.
    2. Confirm the caller's name, callback number, requested service and location.
    3. Check urgency and the caller's actual request rather than relying only on an AI label.
    4. Assign an authorized person to follow up and record the real outcome in your business system.
    5. Tell Star69AI if the receptionist repeatedly misses a question or captures the wrong details.
    Why might a call not have an Intelligence report?

    Some calls may not have a completed provider analysis event, usable transcript or successful AI processing. A report may be delayed or unavailable while a technical issue is investigated. The original call record may still appear in Call activity. Contact Crew Support if reports are consistently missing.

    03 · CALLS & NOTIFICATIONS

    Follow every conversation

    Where can I see calls?

    Open Call activity to review call records available for your business. The portal currently displays the latest 50 calls returned by the service, including available date, caller, status, duration and summary information. Select Details to open the call record.

    What appears depends on whether the call was successfully recorded and analyzed and on the data supplied by the voice provider.

    What are summaries, transcripts and recordings?
    • Summary: a short account of the conversation and its outcome.
    • Transcript: the available text representation of what was said.
    • Recording: audio playback when a recording is available for that call.

    Not every call will necessarily have all three. Availability depends on the call, provider processing, your configuration and applicable recording settings. Review local consent and recording requirements with appropriate counsel before enabling recording.

    Can I email call details to a team member?

    Yes. Open a call’s Details, enter the recipient’s email address and confirm that the recipient is authorized to receive the information before sending. Call details may contain caller information, so only share them with people who need access for your business.

    How do automatic call-summary emails work?

    In Call activity, use “Email call summaries automatically” to enable or disable notifications and enter up to 10 authorized recipient addresses, separated by commas or new lines. Save your preferences. When enabled, the configured email workflow sends available summary details after calls are analyzed.

    Keep the list limited to approved business recipients. If an expected email does not arrive, check the address and spam folder, then contact support with the approximate call time.

    04 · MINUTES & MINUTE VAULT

    Understand your usage

    What counts as a voice minute?

    Your plan includes a monthly allowance of AI voice usage. The portal reports usage for the current billing period. Provider measurement and rounding can differ from the time you spend looking at a call record; use the account usage display and your plan terms as the reference for your service.

    How does the Minute Vault work?

    50% of unused included minutes carry into your Minute Vault while your account remains active and paid. The current month’s included minutes are used first. If you need more, available vaulted minutes are then consumed from the oldest saved balance first (first in, first out).

    The portal shows your saved balance, minutes used from the vault this month and any minutes remaining beyond the included allowance and vault. Future monthly grants follow your then-current plan.

    Do vault minutes have cash value?

    No. Vault minutes are service-use minutes only. They cannot be sold, transferred, refunded, redeemed for cash or applied as a monetary account credit. They are not a separate bank balance.

    What happens to my vault if I change plans or cancel?

    Vault minutes are preserved when you upgrade or downgrade; future included-minute grants follow the new plan. If service is cancelled, the remaining vault balance is forfeited. The vault is available only while the account remains active and paid under the service rules.

    What is overage and will I be charged automatically?

    Overage is voice usage remaining after the current included minutes and available vault minutes have been used. The maximum permitted overage is fixed at 75% of your plan’s included minutes: 225 minutes on Starter, 450 on Growth and 900 on Pro. Calls are assigned a maximum duration from the remaining usage capacity, and new calls are blocked before the ceiling can be exceeded. The portal may show an estimated overage amount based on the plan rate. That display is an estimate, not an invoice or charge.

    Automatic overage billing is available only after you explicitly authorize it and save a card. Star69AI calculates eligible usage and creates Stripe invoice items; this is not a Stripe metered subscription price. Monthly overage is added to your next subscription invoice. Prepaid-plan overage is charged on a separate Stripe invoice. Billable overage is capped at 75% of your included plan minutes. Until card setup and authorization are complete, calls stop after your included minutes and available Minute Vault balance.

    06 · UPGRADES & INTEGRATIONS

    Extend your receptionist when you need more

    What optional improvements can I request?

    The portal may offer requests for custom voice options, additional business knowledge, noise handling, extra safeguards, privacy redaction, SMS messaging, additional numbers, more simultaneous call capacity, a different AI response engine, calendar or CRM workflows and other configuration changes.

    These are requests, not a promise that a feature is already active or included. We confirm technical compatibility, availability, any third-party costs and a written quote before work starts or a charge is made.

    How do upgrades get approved?
    1. Choose an improvement or submit a configuration request.
    2. Star69AI reviews the request and confirms feasibility, pricing and any third-party pass-through costs.
    3. You review the written quote. Nothing is changed or charged merely because you submitted a request.
    4. Where the portal provides an approval action, use it only after reviewing the quote and its scope.
    5. Our Crew completes and tests the approved work, then updates the request status.
    How does SMS messaging work?

    Business texting may require identity verification, carrier registration, documented customer opt-in, approved message examples and opt-out/help instructions. Approval and timing depend on carriers and providers. Never send marketing or other texts to people who have not provided the required consent. Submitting the SMS details form does not activate texting.

    Do not enter a Social Security number or full tax ID in the portal form. If a tax ID is needed, we will arrange an appropriate secure verification step.

    What is custom voice cloning?

    Voice cloning is an optional request subject to provider availability, identity and consent checks, commercial-use terms, language support and technical compatibility. The speaker must be you or someone who has explicitly authorized the intended use. Do not submit another person’s voice without their permission. We confirm the provider, recording requirements and quote before proceeding.

    07 · PRIVACY & SECURITY

    Protect your business and callers

    Who can see my portal information?

    The portal is designed to show your business workspace to your signed-in account and authorized Star69AI administrators. Account access and customer data permissions are enforced by the service, not just by hiding screens in the browser. Keep your sign-in secure and sign out on shared devices.

    How should I handle caller information?

    Only collect information needed to handle the business inquiry. Limit call summaries, transcripts and recordings to authorized staff. Avoid forwarding sensitive caller details to personal inboxes or broad distribution lists. Tell Star69AI if your access, team or retention requirements change.

    Are calls recorded?

    Recording and transcription availability depends on your configuration, provider behavior and applicable law. Businesses are responsible for determining and providing any notices or consent required for their callers. Ask Star69AI to confirm the recording settings for your account before launch.

    08 · HELP & SUPPORT

    Get the right help quickly

    How do I request a change or get technical help?

    Use Crew Support for billing questions, technical problems, concerns and general requests. Use the Receptionist page for detailed changes to greetings, language, business knowledge, routing, voice or workflows. Include the approximate time of an affected call and what you expected to happen. Do not include passwords, card numbers or unnecessary sensitive caller data.

    How do I track a support request?

    After submitting a support ticket, it appears in your support history with its category, status, priority and any reply from our Crew. Use Refresh to check for updates. Statuses may include Open, In progress, Waiting on you, Resolved and Closed.

    What should I do if calls stop reaching my business?

    Submit a Crew Support request with Urgent priority if your receptionist is unavailable or calls are not reaching the expected destination. Include the business number affected, approximate start time and a safe example of the issue. If you have an agreed backup number or business continuity procedure, use it while we investigate.
