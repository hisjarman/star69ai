LEGAL INTAKE WORKFLOWS

Professional intake. Clear boundaries.

A receptionist can organize administrative intake without evaluating legal merits, giving advice or implying that the firm has accepted a matter.

New-client inquiries

Capture name, contact method, preferred callback time and broad matter category without assessing legal merits.

Existing-client calls

Route to the appropriate team or collect a callback message using firm-approved identifiers and processes.

Consultation requests

Record preferred times and pass requests to the firm's calendar or intake team. Never promise representation or confirm a consultation without system confirmation.

Confidentiality boundaries

Avoid inviting callers to disclose detailed confidential facts before the firm's conflict and intake process allows it.
