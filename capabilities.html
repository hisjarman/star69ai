THE CAPABILITY LIBRARY

What can your AI
actually do?

Choose the jobs you want your receptionist to handle. Each workflow is scoped to your business, plan, provider support and approved permissions.

01

Answer & understand

Make every conversation more useful.

◉
INBOUND VOICE

Answer calls around the clock

Greet callers, understand why they're calling, answer approved questions and capture the details your team needs—even when your office is closed.

⌕
BUSINESS KNOWLEDGE

Answer business-specific questions

Use information you approve, such as services, hours, locations, service areas, policies and frequently asked questions.

◎
CUSTOMER CONTEXT

Recognize returning customers

Look up a caller against authorized customer records and use relevant information within the permissions and data available to that workflow.

✧
MULTILINGUAL

Support multilingual conversations

Configure supported languages and voice behavior to help serve more callers. Language availability and quality depend on the selected voice and configuration.

02

Book & coordinate

Turn a phone conversation into a confirmed next step.

▦
APPOINTMENTS

Schedule and manage appointments

Check connected calendar availability, request or confirm appointments, and handle changes or cancellations when the connected system supports those actions.

✳
RESTAURANT WORKFLOWS

Handle restaurant reservations

Check available tables, create or change reservations and communicate booking details when an authorized restaurant reservation system or supported API is connected.

⌖
SERVICE DISPATCH

Collect and route service requests

Gather job details, service address, urgency and preferred timing, then route the request according to your team's approved dispatch and escalation rules.

⇄
HUMAN HANDOFF

Transfer calls to the right person

Route callers to a team member, department or on-call contact using the business rules and transfer destinations you approve.

03

Follow up & recover

Keep the conversation moving after the call.

↗
LEAD QUALIFICATION

Qualify new opportunities

Ask relevant questions, identify the caller's needs, capture contact details and prepare a structured handoff for your sales or service team.

↻

Follow up on missed calls

Use approved SMS or callback workflows to reconnect with eligible missed callers and help recover opportunities that might otherwise be lost.

▤

Send confirmations and reminders

Send permitted confirmations, reminders, status updates and follow-up messages through configured messaging providers.

Plan supported connections to CRMs, calendars, field-service software and custom systems. We verify provider support, access scope and testing before activation.

▥
MISSION CONTROL

MANAGED SERVICE

Request changes to greetings, questions, business knowledge and routing. Star69AI manages configuration, testing and approved updates with you.

YOUR SYSTEMS. YOUR PERMISSIONS.

Connect the tools
you already use.

Star69AI is designed to work with your existing business systems where supported. You can request a connection through your client portal, describe what the AI should be allowed to do, and let our team review the provider, access scope, costs and test plan.

  • Use the least access needed for the agreed workflow.
  • Keep customer records separated by business account.
  • Never send passwords, API keys or secret credentials through a request form.
  • Confirm the connection and test it before relying on it with customers.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Configured for your business.
Never promised blindly.

Not every capability is automatically included or active for every customer. Features depend on your plan, the connected provider, account permissions, business rules, applicable consent and testing. Some workflows require a supported third-party system or custom integration.

Star69AI does not make unapproved bookings, invent availability, guarantee lead recovery, provide professional or medical advice, or bypass your team's approval rules. We'll confirm what is supported before describing a workflow as ready to use.
