    CLEAR RULES FOR IMPORTANT CALLS

    A front desk with clear handoff rules.

    You decide what the receptionist can answer, when a person should take over and what happens if a transfer cannot be completed. We document and test those instructions before launch.

    01 / LIVE TRANSFERS

    Send the right calls to a person

    Choose the transfer number, hours and call types that should reach you or your team. We test the configured route and agree on the fallback if nobody answers.

    02 / ESCALATION RULES

    Define what counts as urgent

    Set instructions for active leaks, safety concerns, priority customers or other situations that need a human. The receptionist follows your rules; it does not dispatch emergency crews on its own.

    03 / RECORDINGS & REVIEW

    Know what was said

    Where recording is enabled and supported, we confirm caller notice, access and retention settings for your configuration. Authorized users can review available recordings and call details in the client portal.

    04 / SAFE FALLBACKS

    No guessing on critical details

    We define approved answers and what to do when the receptionist is unsure. It should collect a callback request or use the agreed handoff—not invent prices, availability, warranties or technical advice.

    These are configuration safeguards, not a promise of zero risk or uninterrupted carrier service. Transfer, recording and portal features depend on provider availability, customer settings and applicable requirements.
