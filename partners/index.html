RECURRING PARTNER COMMISSIONS

Earn more as your
referral network grows.

Star69AI rewards approved partners for introducing businesses that become active, paying subscription customers. Your commission tier is based on the number of active referred customers and applies to eligible subscription revenue actually collected after customer discounts.

01–04 ACTIVE CUSTOMERS20%

Partner tier

Earn 20% of eligible collected subscription revenue while each referred customer remains active and paid.

05–14 ACTIVE CUSTOMERS25%

Growth tier

Reach five active referred customers to qualify for a 25% commission rate on eligible collected subscription revenue.

15+ ACTIVE CUSTOMERS30%

Premier tier

Reach fifteen active referred customers to qualify for the 30% commission rate.

What commission is based on

  • Actual eligible monthly subscription payments received, after any customer subscription discount.
  • Commission is not calculated on list price, setup fees or unpaid amounts.
  • Partner attribution is intended to remain active for 90 days where tracking and verification succeed.

Exclusions and payout timing

  • No commission on setup, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits, phone-number rental, usage overage or carrier/third-party pass-through charges.
  • Each eligible commission has a 30-day validation hold.
  • PayPal payouts are reviewed monthly after the $50 minimum payout balance is reached.

The one-time $99 setup fee is not discounted by partner referrals and is not commissionable. A partner referral may offer 15% off eligible monthly subscription payments for the first 12 months only. That offer cannot be combined with AI Challenge rewards or six-/twelve-month prepaid plans. Standard referral links preserve attribution and commission eligibility without a customer discount.

A TRUST-BASED PARTNERSHIP

Professional introductions.
Responsible promotion.

We keep individual partner economics and referral tools private. The public expectations are straightforward:

Be transparent

  • Disclose your material connection to Star69AI when promoting the service.
  • Describe the managed service accurately and use approved claims.
  • Never guarantee leads, revenue, call outcomes or business results.

Respect people and businesses

  • No spam, deceptive claims, impersonation or misleading earnings statements.
  • Follow applicable advertising, privacy and anti-spam requirements.
  • Do not submit self-referrals, duplicate accounts or fraudulent orders.

Approval comes first

  • Submitting an application does not automatically enroll you.
  • Approved partners receive private access to the Partner Center.
  • Review the full current partner agreement before accepting or sharing links.

Questions before applying?

  • Use the application form to describe your audience and approach.
  • Partner support is available at support@star69ai.com.
  • Partner-specific terms are disclosed in the authenticated console.
