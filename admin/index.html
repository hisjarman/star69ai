RESEARCH DRAFT · VERIFY BEFORE QUOTING
Purpose: price optional services fairly from actual incremental cost and real labor—not arbitrary competitor undercutting. This page is internal guidance, not a live price book or an authorization to activate/bill an add-on.

Pricing rules

  • Internal labor guide: $20/hour ($5 per 15 minutes; $10 per 30 minutes; $20 per hour).
  • Separate one-time creation/setup costs from recurring usage and maintenance.
  • Do not invent a monthly fee when there is no recurring cost or ongoing service.
  • Allocate shared platform subscriptions fairly across active users; never assign the entire shared fee to every customer.
  • Use actual incremental provider costs. Show the customer the billing unit, usage limits and any recurring component before approval.
  • Current terms policy: monthly third-party charges above $2 are passed through at verified cost without markup; monthly costs of $2 or less are absorbed. One-time provider charges are separately itemized and approved in advance.
  • Do not charge separately for features already included in Starter, Growth, Pro or Flex. Standard call handling, routing, recordings/transcripts, summaries, client portal, routine managed setup and included reporting are not duplicate add-ons.
