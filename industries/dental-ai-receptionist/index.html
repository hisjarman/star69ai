DENTAL CALL WORKFLOWS

Make the first conversation more useful.

Configure administrative call handling around the practice's real policies. The receptionist should gather context, not practice dentistry.

New-patient inquiries

Capture contact details, preferred location, broad appointment need and the best time for a team callback.

Appointment requests

Collect preferred days and times or pass a request into an approved scheduling workflow. Never imply a booking is confirmed unless the connected system confirms it.

Office information

Share approved hours, location, payment information and preparation instructions supplied by the practice.

Urgent or clinical concerns

Use practice-approved escalation instructions. Do not diagnose, triage clinically or replace emergency services.

Privacy and clinical boundaries

Dental practices should define privacy, consent, minimum-necessary data collection, retention, access and escalation with qualified compliance and legal advisers. Do not send protected health information to a system unless the practice has verified the complete vendor and contractual requirements, including any required business associate agreement. The receptionist must not diagnose, triage clinically or replace emergency services.
